# Contracts and limits

The authoritative typed contracts are `operational_quality.pharmacy_handoff`. Extra event fields are rejected. Inputs use only synthetic `RX-DEMO-nnn`, `EP-nn`, `FILL-nn`, `PA-DEMO-nnn` and source-qualified `EV-nnn` IDs. There are no patient, medication, dose or clinical free-text fields. This is data minimization, not a PHI detector: a synthetic marker cannot prove data is fictional. Do not provide real data, including in approval artifacts.

Scope is one to twenty explicit fictional cases within seven days, maximum 1,000 events and a configured output-byte budget. JSON reads reuse the existing bounded reader. Required source manifests are `intake_feed`, `pa_feed`, `pharmacy_feed`, each with exact scoped IDs, count, availability, truncation, completeness watermark and clock uncertainty. These are generic mock source roles, not vendor connectors or a claim that any company uses them. No live pharmacy adapter is implemented. Existing Datadog/PostgreSQL capabilities elsewhere in the repository are not invoked by this profile.

A versioned policy requires a content-bound approved decision and exact source quote; its effective interval must contain the entire audit scope. Local approval authenticity and suitability remain human checks. Configurable seconds for acknowledgment, ownership, clarification and approval-next-step signals are administrative review thresholds only. Feed lag and repeated-handoff count are also policy settings.

Events need aware occurrence and receipt times. Missing, out-of-scope or incoherent times, unresolved links, duplicate source-qualified IDs, conflicting case IDs or uncertain ordering yield unknown. Ambiguous IDs quarantine affected cases, or all cases if identity cannot be established. Quarantine never reproduces the raw payload. All three feeds must be usable; a gap suppresses interpretation rather than implying nonperformance. Absence ages stop at the earliest feed watermark and subtract clock uncertainty. Counts and feed coverage are source assertions, not independently verified population coverage.

Each episode has exactly one start: initial intake, or a reopening explicitly linked to the prior episode's terminal record. Multiple closures or post-closure work without a new episode are unknown. Case cancellation/closure suppresses pending-work signals in that episode. Partial or completed fill records do not close the case. Fill cancellation suppresses pending work only for that fill; repeated-handoff findings remain historical observations. Reused fill IDs and inconsistent lifecycle evidence require human review; no clinical fill-state machine is implemented.

Acknowledgment and owner assignment must link the specific handoff and destination queue; a recorded owner on acknowledgment can satisfy ownership. Clarification responses must link the request. Off-system resolution must explicitly link the work item and matching episode/fill. Unlinked resolution cannot silently clear a signal. Next steps link a recorded PA approval; a fill-specific approval requires the same fill, while a case-scoped approval may link a fill-specific next step. This means only an observed administrative next step, not that all fills or dispensing are complete.

PA updates require explicit `supersedes_ref` with matching case, episode, request and fill. Without it, conflicting recorded statuses remain a reconciliation finding, not a “latest timestamp wins” determination. Repeated active approvals or mismatched fill scopes for the same request are unknown until explicitly reconciled. Approval is a source label, never an independently verified insurance determination.

Output includes source-qualified refs, structured timelines, source urgency labels, policy hash, approval provenance, feed coverage, quarantine reasons and report hash. No clinical risk score is computed. Source refs identify mock records, not independently authenticated records or live links. Host review is advisory and cannot mechanically prevent a separately privileged host from taking actions; the CLI itself has no network/production action path.

No FHIR, NCPDP or other standards mappings are implemented or compliance claims made. Production adaptation, real-data privacy/access handling and vendor source mappings are outside this synthetic first version.
